{# -- detector -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Face detector detection only

Training a YOLO face detector on your verified boxes improves how reliably faces are found in your own imagery. It does not improve identification — telling people apart stays with the embedding pipeline above, and a detector run changes no recognition result on its own. Better detection does feed it: a face that is never found is never embedded.
