Every read is measured whether or not a floor is set, so the floors are chosen from these columns. OCR confidence is the weakest of them: a smeared 60-pixel plate scores high because the model is sure about the characters it interpolated. Plate width and sharpness say whether the picture could have carried the answer at all, and the weakest character is what a mean confidence hides.
