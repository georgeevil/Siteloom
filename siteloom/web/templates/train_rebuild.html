What a rebuild does

Drops {{ plan.total_points }} vectors across {{ plan.identities }} identities, then re-embeds {{ plan.recoverable_points }} of them from their stored crops under the current settings. {{ plan.unrecoverable_points }} vectors have no crop on disk anywhere (API-enrolled, or predating crop provenance) and cannot be recovered.

Names, identities and crops all survive — vectors are derived data. While the rebuild runs, identities read as unenrolled and recognition is degraded; an interrupted rebuild resumes (siteloom identity rebuild --resume) and never leaves a mixed-space gallery behind.
